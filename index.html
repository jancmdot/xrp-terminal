How impact is rated

Every item gets a tier from a fixed rubric. The same factors apply to every item, so the rating doesn't depend on how excited the post sounds.

  • Source tier: primary record, established outlet, smaller outlet, or unvetted account
  • New information, or already scheduled and known
  • Size relative to XRP's daily volume, ETF assets or float
  • Confirmation status: confirmed, reported, rumor, speculative
  • Market positioning at the time (leverage, funding)

Where the probability comes from

Every item is logged with its time, theme and source tier. The pipeline then measures XRP's move against BTC over 1h, 4h and 24h. Once a theme has 30 logged events, its items show the historical share that moved more than ±3% versus BTC within 24 hours. Until then you see the rubric tier only, marked RUBRIC.
