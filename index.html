How ratings and probabilities are made

How impact is rated

Grok fills in the same fields for every item, and the tier is calculated from them with fixed points, so it doesn't depend on how excited a post sounds:

  • Source tier: primary record, established outlet, smaller outlet, or unvetted account
  • Confirmation: confirmed, reported, rumor, speculative
  • New information, or already known or scheduled
  • Size relative to XRP's daily volume, ETF flows or float
  • Whether it's specifically about XRP

Items without real size rate low at most. Market positioning (funding, open interest) is given to Grok as context for the rationale but isn't scored.

Where the probability comes from

Every item is logged with its time, theme and source tier. The pipeline then measures XRP's move against BTC over 1h, 4h and 24h. Once a theme has 30 logged events, its items show the historical share that moved more than ±3% versus BTC within 24 hours. Until then you see the rubric tier only, marked RUBRIC.
